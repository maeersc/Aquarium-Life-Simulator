## Shared technical findings and resolution

The original clownfish frames contained eyes. Silhouette-based fitting shifted head coordinates as fins changed, and repeated downsampling could lose small pupil detail. Head registration, a canonical artist eye component and final-size cached sampling correct this. Components are sprite-local and transform with the head. Odd-width mirroring is pixel-aligned; canvas transforms/compositing are restored between animals. Facing follows displayed target displacement with a threshold, preserving direction during small jitter and avoiding backward interpolation.

Every existing species now uses the same rendering boundary. The runtime simulator's existing velocity/heading behavior is retained. Dedicated authored left-side sprites are supported when future asymmetric species require them. Current profiles use mirrored side views.

Legacy artwork still reuses body templates and lacks most explicit scientific-name fields. Normal swim uses four frames, idle reuses them at a slower cadence, and turning uses stable mirrored endpoints. Death freezes an inverted frame; dedicated illness/feeding/turn-transition/decomposition sets remain unfinished. Fictional documentation proposals Emberjaw Leviathan, Abyssal Crownfish and Prismheart Ray remain PLANNED with no runtime IDs.
