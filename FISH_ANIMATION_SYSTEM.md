# Marine fish animation architecture — v4.0.3

This is a 2D presentation service. It never advances biological time, consumes RNG, changes nutrition/health, spawns eggs, modifies inherited traits or writes player saves.

`assets/animation/fish-registry.json` assigns every registered marine fish to an explicit artistic motion family and three stage rates, with tail root/head protection and reviewed eye corrections. `build.py` regenerates `js/fish-animation-data.js`; tests reject unassigned new marine species. These rates/regions are artistic game calibrations, not experimentally verified swimming biomechanics. Five art-only reference fish are excluded; five held clownfish remain acquisition-held.

`AL.FishAnimation.update(f, rendererPosition, dt, desiredFacing)` stores its clock, beat, bank, state and 320-ms turn solely on `Renderer.positions`. Real velocity sets propulsion frequency. Actual Eat/Rest/Hide and existing reproductive labels select motion variants. Conditions become visual only after their existing onset and severity threshold, with carrier-only states not automatically exposing a symptom. Respiratory conditions, irritation and weakness have different motions; presentation never diagnoses or treats a fish.

`source` preserves asset priority and approved natural adult Ocellaris benchmark frames. Other adult clownfish retain their four authored swim frames. Young clownfish, base marine fish and both expansion batches reuse their authored stage/state sources. Banggai's early display selects the existing developed-juvenile artwork, preserving the mouthbrooding simulation. `AL.ExpansionArt` applies central eye metadata before scaling and retains native-facing normalization. One Blue Assessor baby eye registration was corrected in raw left-facing coordinates; 25 v2.9 fish gained their previously absent living eye registrations for baby, juvenile and adult. Original PNG bytes remain unchanged.

Individual `AL.Patterns.sprite` output enters articulation before movement, so body bars and inherited identities are never independently regenerated per fin frame. Approved authored adult clownfish cycles bypass procedural deformation. Other poses use final-size rasters and thin column strips: the caudal hinge is fixed, tail columns shift by at most a few integer pixels, and authored upper/lower fin pixels flex slightly about an unchanged core. The head region and eye patch stay unchanged across procedural phases. Four-pixel transparent padding contains excursions. All image sampling uses nearest-neighbor. There is no synthetic eye, fin repaint, whole-body squash or 3D renderer.

Poses use a global 768-entry LRU. Source registration/raster caches are weakly keyed and limited per source/size. Image decoding and alpha readback happen outside the per-frame hot path on cache misses. Renderer scratch arrays retain sorted fish references and hitboxes, positions prune by frame stamp, and offscreen marine fish skip sprite/rig work. Conservative hitbox padding covers fin/turn motion. Selection expires after three real seconds; inspector lifetime remains independent. Biological outcomes are tested equal after different render counts with the same simulated interval; the test controls diagnostic receipt wall time rather than hiding differences in health or account values.

The standalone `fish-animation-review.html` loads art/model definitions without the game save/startup/main scripts. It constructs only throwaway specimen objects and supports all 94 IDs, designer varieties, three stages, both sides and state studies. It is not a store, breeding authorization, disease diagnosis or a way to acquire held livestock.

### Actual in-tank scale and early fin-layer gaps

All 282 fish/stage combinations are also checked at the renderer's actual size, including the individual's size gene. The core guard now scales down to one scene pixel instead of covering all tiny fin-fold pixels. Separate outer-fin motion remains unsupported by four retained early-stage sources at the relevant size: Hi-fin Red-banded Goby (caudal-fold-only source), Whitebonnet Clownfish, Lubbock's Fairy Wrasse and Redspotted Blenny (outer fin pixels unresolved below 32 scene pixels). The renderer retains caudal fin/fold motion and protects body/yolk pixels; it does not manufacture paired fins. These are explicit art gaps, not passed independent-fin animation requirements. Larger applicable outer-fin poses and every stage's tail/caudal fold are tested. All four species remain class B pending dedicated early-fin artwork.


Full authored turning, occluded pectoral clean plates, species-specific feeding/spawning/mouth-detail keyframes and anatomically reviewed early stages remain art work. Current turns are brief banked side-profile transitions with a single mirrored visible eye; no authored yaw or true opposite-side asymmetric markings are claimed. Existing fin/body layers cannot reveal pixels that were never painted. All 94 are therefore classified B (partial), with zero falsely labeled fully authored/complete species. Sixty-three lack dedicated adult swim-cycle keyframes; 31 retain them. See FISH_ANIMATION_AUDIT.md for every species, actual tests and pending art.

## Recorded source cleanup

The juvenile Orange-Spotted Filefish atlas contains a disconnected 159-pixel duplicate fin island below the body. Its explicit sourceMasks rectangle is applied only to that living stage by the shared art adapter, after facing normalization. The original PNG remains byte-identical; the living body/actual fins are not repainted. Other hosts and death frames keep their existing source policy.
