## Bounds and safeguards

- Immediate resale stays below original retail even at the maximum allowed quality and demand modifiers.
- Local buying budget is 1,200 AC/day with eight fish sales/day. Species demand drops with sales and recovers gradually.
- Adult load is reserved for both current fish and developing broods. Capacity cannot be bypassed by filling a tank while eggs develop.
- Living organisms are placed into an actual simulated aquarium. No indefinite fish inventory exists.
- Cutting sales require healthy growth ≥1.5×, remove 0.4× growth, pay 15 AC and consume buyer budget.
- Showcase income is 90 AC at most once per game day, requiring three healthy residents and a full safe day.
- Quest rewards are finite, prerequisite-gated and single claim. Experience cannot be earned by repeatedly clicking a completed entry.
- Brood sizes and gene ranges are bounded. The 60-day breeder case demonstrates no exponential income.
- Stock replenishes in limited daily increments. Purchases check both stock and funds, and failed actions roll back.
- Energy shortfalls cannot create negative balances. An explicitly logged emergency allowance keeps essential welfare equipment running.

## Remaining balance risks

- These are single-seed deterministic scenarios. Repeated-seed mortality, rare-market risk and optimal-player progression distributions need a larger study.
- Breeding alone lost 157 AC over 60 days in the isolated operating scenario. First-time quest rewards and recurring showcases make the broader loop more rewarding; specialist breeding contracts are a future priority.
- The utility allowance intentionally makes an empty wallet forgiving. It prevents debt spirals but can subsidize abandoned or oversized collections.
- Showcase rewards can eventually finance expansion. Long-term showcase income at maximum tank ownership has not been exhaustively analyzed.
- The advanced collector setup cost and outcome are measured; the time required for a beginner to earn that setup naturally is not.
- Selling members of a social school can leave a smaller group and cause stress; sales warn players to consider group welfare but are not universally blocked.
- No real-money economy exists. Manually editing a local JSON file is outside normal gameplay exploit prevention.
