# Compatible breeding-pair offers

AL.PairOffers consumes the existing AL.Breeding profiles. Every eligible implemented fish listing has a configured 0.05 chance, fixed for its legitimate listing refresh. The current regression enumerates all 29 supported catalog fish: `clown`, `banggai`, `amphiprion-percula`, `amphiprion-biaculeatus`, `amphiprion-clarkii`, `amphiprion-frenatus`, `amphiprion-melanopus`, `amphiprion-ephippium`, `amphiprion-perideraion`, `amphiprion-sandaracinos`, `amphiprion-akallopisos`, `amphiprion-polymnus`, `amphiprion-sebae`, `amphiprion-akindynos`, `amphiprion-allardi`, `amphiprion-bicinctus`, `amphiprion-chrysopterus`, `amphiprion-nigripes`, `amphiprion-omanensis`, `amphiprion-chagosensis`, `amphiprion-chrysogaster`, `amphiprion-fuscocaudatus`, `amphiprion-latifasciatus`, `amphiprion-rubrocinctus`, `amphiprion-tricinctus`, `amphiprion-barberi`, `amphiprion-pacificus`, `chalk-bass`, `sharknose-goby`. Other species are explicitly omitted because their full reproductive/rearing configuration is not verified in this build, not because the game claims they cannot breed in nature. School/group spawning and acquisition-held content are not forced into invented conventional pairs. Unverified designer inheritance is held; the existing supported Snowflake reference line may use the offer.

Opening details prepares and locally saves a real offer before showing it. A rejection-sampled deterministic hash of profile plus daily/dealer listing and species/variety produces0–9999; values below 500 receive the offer. No biological/store RNG is consumed, and clicking, closing, reload or unavailable storage cannot reroll a listing. Normal fallback products refresh by authoritative game day; daily/dealer listings additionally use their actual refresh IDs. Old unpurchased entries are pruned only after their day; purchase receipts remain durable.

A successful offer reserves two distinct permanent fish IDs and privately generated individual genes, mature ages, health, patterns and biological roles. Protandrous clownfish use mature female/male roles and relative size; Chalk Bass retain simultaneous-hermaphrodite roles. Offers say Compatible Breeding Pair, never Established Bonded Pair. Store-partner metadata is not a fictional past courtship/spawning record. Existing autonomous breeding still requires health, nutrition, low stress, maturity, water, nest/habitat, rearing food and stocking headroom; purchase does not spawn a brood.

Each specimen uses the existing legitimate standard-adult retail/dealer unit quote; total is their sum, with **no pair discount or markup**. Actual individual asset values remain the authoritative condition/genetic valuations, separate from fixed purchase prices and resale. Healthy dealer listings retain their original listing provenance and market rules; unsuitable dealer conditions do not advertise a breeding pair.

Pair reporting runs the original two-fish StoreRules compatibility, then the shared receiving/medical authority sequentially on a disposable destination with the first incoming adult included before the second. It rechecks combined adult bioload, species/water, actual volume, keeper access, stock, active restricted treatment/quarantine and habitat. It cannot waive a species safeguard. All owned destinations retain their explanations in the original purchase interface. The aquarium selection remains selected after the purchase.

One atomic action verifies the current saved offer, exact price and both specimens, debits once, reduces two units of global and actual listing stock, transfers those exact IDs into the chosen tank, saves the purchase/receiving receipt and removes the preview copies. A purchased or stale offer cannot grant again. Successful ownership feeds the same first-species authority, so two fish of one new species award 50 XP/50 AC once, not twice. Save details are in ECONOMY_UPDATE_SAVE_SCHEMA.md.
