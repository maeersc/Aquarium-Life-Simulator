    What still differs

    • The generated fish is rounder, with stronger black edging and more pronounced fin shapes than a natural ocellaris profile.
    • At normal game size, scale and fin details become small clusters; the 128-pixel asset primarily benefits enlargement.
    • Four swim poses are available. Minor changes to the body and band shapes between frames still need cleanup.
    • Turning shows mirrored endpoints; death shows a frozen inverted pose. Neither is a finished dedicated animation.
