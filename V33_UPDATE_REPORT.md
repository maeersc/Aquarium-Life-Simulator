# Aquarium Life — v3.1–v3.3 implementation report

Playable v3.3 / consolidated 31-section MDP v3.6, 2026-10-14. Scope: the three uploaded coral/trading, finance and research specifications, archived verbatim under planning/v3.1-v3.3. Development remains saltwater-only.

## Implemented systems

Coral Fragging is inside existing Equipment. Reusable Kit (220 AC), Cutters (180 AC), Saw (420 AC), plugs (30 AC/10) and mounting supplies (25 AC/10) reuse the existing inventory, stock, purchasing and financial service. Frag Rack remains the existing 100-AC mounted object. Eligibility on actual coral inspectors checks maturity/size, at least 85% health (stricter for some anatomy), tissue, disease/treatment, water/light/flow, equipment, consumables, free capacity, safe mount and recovery. The confirmation shows parent/fragment size/value and risk, lets the player select method and mounting point, and changes nothing on dismissal.

Twelve existing coral records have calibrated propagation profiles, using kit, cutters or saw as appropriate. Parent mass/health/tissue decrease; one independent child retains biological type, rarity, cloned genes and root lineage. It receives a unique ID, supported mount, independent health/growth/death, temporary stress and establishment period. Parents need regrowth and recovery before another cut. Immediate total parent-plus-child asset value cannot exceed the pre-cut parent. New frags cannot be sold before establishment. Legacy hammer anatomy and two unsupported nonphotosynthetic records remain held rather than assuming a safe method. No new coral species is added; no real cutting protocol is provided.

The regular Aquarium Store and Deep Market share individual sales for fish, corals and invertebrates. Diving regular offer is exactly floor(Deep reference × 0.60), from the same specimen calculation even when not wanted. Other origins keep their established central offer formula. Deep buys require Reputation 5, matching biological-ID Wanted Livestock request, condition, opening/access and existing budget/sale limits. The daily wanted board has six persisted requests, separate from exactly four rotating dealer livestock stock slots. Reopening does not reroll them. Sales remove the actual selected-tank individual once, update assets/demand/saturation/request count, record the real receipt and preserve destination.

The AC header is a native keyboard button opening Financial Overview. Actual wallet, opening/income/expenses/net/closing, current-day/7/30/all-time periods, newest-first filters, detailed receipts, twenty-row pages, actual daily chart and explicit archival are integrated. One authoritative financial service covers all main-game AC sources/sinks. New profiles start at the requested 5,000 AC; existing wallets remain unchanged with a labeled migration opening balance. No fictitious history or payout is created. See FINANCIAL_TRANSACTIONS.md for sources, schema, reconciliation, archival and import behavior.

Saltwater Store Research dynamically indexes 94 marine fish records. Exactly 93 real biological species are eligible for a permanent 5-AC unlock, independent of ownership; the fictional entry has a free preview. Designer morphs share biological unlocks. Search/status filters/rarity sorting, preview disclosure, atomic payment/unlock, permanent account progress, shared Field Journal Encyclopedia reports, medical-guide routing and optional first-research guidance are implemented. Research does not bypass livestock acquisition holds. All ten dossier sections exist; game calibrations are clearly separated from verified biology.
