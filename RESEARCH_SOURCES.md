## Coral propagation and abstracted hazards

NOAA Coral Disease & Health Consortium, Coral Reproduction: https://cdhc.noaa.gov/coral-biology/coral-reproduction/ supports asexual reproduction/clone identity. NOAA restoration overview https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/education/tutorial_corals/coral15_restoring-corals.html and NOAA Fisheries https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/national/habitat-conservation/restoring-coral-reefs support fragment propagation and attachment as broad concepts. These are not evidence for the game's numeric recovery, risk, age, tissue-loss or pricing thresholds.

CDC MMWR, aquarium zoanthid exposures: https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/mm6431a4.htm supports the potential palytoxin hazard from some zoanthids. Gameplay abstracts handling and supplies no real cutting instructions. Legacy hammer wall/branch identity and unsupported nonphotosynthetic methods remain held. No source is claimed to validate those methods.

All URLs are stored as evidence text. Runtime gameplay needs no internet. The existing Disease & Treatment Guide remains authoritative for game diagnosis, host restrictions and treatment; no universal reef-safe treatment or species-specific susceptibility is asserted without supporting evidence.
