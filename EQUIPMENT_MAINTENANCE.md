# Equipment maintenance and Replace Part

In an installed equipment inspector choose Maintenance or Replace Part. They are separate actions. Maintenance cleans/calibrates accessible components, removes maintenance burden and may clear a temporary malfunction. It preserves physical condition, true age, unrelated part wear and established filter bacteria. Its established30% catalog/rating maintenance coefficient remains unchanged.

Replace Part shows supported catalog entries, exact rated fit, worn condition, new-part price and matching owned quantity. Buy & replace charges exactly the same price as the rated new part in Aquarium Store. Use owned part consumes one matching quantity with no second charge. It renews that part to100%, preserves the unique device/chassis/age/other parts, recalculates output and clears only a matching supported fault. The original repairEquipment compatibility action uses this same catalog path; the old deficit-based discounted component repair and legacy-zero chassis restoration are superseded. Sealed or unlisted assemblies and zero-condition/beyond-repair chassis require complete physical replacement. No device is automatically restored or deleted.

Supported parts/prices are solely in EQUIPMENT_PART_CATALOG.md / AL.Data.parts. Store purchases go to Inventory. Stocked spares do not operate a tank, and whole-aquarium sales exclude them. Actual maintenance/failures/replacements appear in Tank History and per-device history. Thermostats stay in individual installed heater/chiller controls. Do not restore standalone Temperature Control or Overnight Readiness panels.

Intervals and multi-year aging are preserved: skimmers90 game days/30 real hours; feeders120/40; filters, flow, chillers, air and dosing180/60; heaters, LED fixtures and containment365/121.7. Recommendations occur at 70% maintenance and urgent warnings at 30%. Lighting ages by actual powered schedule, normalized to10h/day. Replacement does not roll a new major expiration date. Existing damaged condition, unknown older age, ownership and all historical records are retained. See EQUIPMENT_DURABILITY_BALANCE_REPORT.md for formulas, service-life distributions and limitations, and ECONOMY_UPDATE_REPORT.md for current executed regressions.
