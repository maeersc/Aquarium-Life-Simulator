# Equipment maintenance

Marine equipment has three separate concepts: physical condition, maintenance cleanliness and malfunction state. Inspect an installed unit in Equipment → Tank Care to see current percentages, tracked service age, component wear and actual operating efficiency. Heater/chiller thermostat controls remain in that individual inspector. The standalone Temperature Control and Overnight Readiness panels remain absent.

Maintain removes contamination and restores maintenance performance; it does not rejuvenate the chassis or reset its age. A filter's established biological bacteria are preserved. Temporary malfunctions can be cleared by maintenance; an identified repair-required fault needs the component repair button. Repair retains the physical ID and tracked age. Beyond-repair gear supplies no life support: remove it to Inventory and install an owned compatible replacement. Removal retains all state; stored gear cannot support an aquarium.

Recommendations occur at 70% maintenance (the type-specific interval), with urgent warnings at 30%. Intervals are 90 game days/30 real hours for skimmers; 120/40 for feeders; 180/60 for filters, circulation, chillers, aeration and dosing; 365/121.7 for heaters, LEDs and containment. Actual lamp on-time governs aging, normalized to the usual 10-hour daily lighting schedule. Maintenance can be done early below 95% or to clear an identified temporary fault. It is unnecessary above 95% on a working unit.

Service prices use the existing catalog/rating fee coefficient and maintenance deficit; repair uses the failed component's deficit (at least 25%). See the balance report for exact formulas and probabilistic service-life distributions. No fixed failure date is shown. Tank History → Equipment maintenance & failures contains actual service, repair, warning and failure records. Each inspector also shows that physical device's retained history, including earlier tank assignments.

Existing condition and ownership are preserved. Unknown earlier age is labeled unknown. No automatic restoration or compensation occurs. Browser/local export saves retain the new counters, histories and failure thresholds.
