# Sell Entire Aquarium

Aquarium Store → Sell → Sell Entire Aquarium lists every owned aquarium, volume, residents by category, installed devices, rocks/decorations and an estimated buyback. Freshwater entries remain unavailable under the scope lock. Review opens an itemized quote with exact total, conditions, identity and all blockers. The required warning is: "You are about to sell this aquarium and all eligible contents. This action cannot be undone." A separate required checkbox and confirmation submit follow selection. Quotes recheck at commit; changed payout/contents require review, while ordinary swimming/rendering does not expire an unchanged monetary quote.

AL.AquariumSale.quote is the single bundle authority and consumes AL.Valuation plus the existing livestock/rock quote services. Shell buyback is floor(base shell asset value×0.50), newly defined using the same half-value physical-asset convention already used by equipment, ordinary rocks and decor. Equipment uses existing rated purchase calibration×condition×0.50, rounded down per item; failed-beyond-repair chassis is explicitly zero-value salvage. Ordinary rocks, sand, racks and decorations reuse their physical valuation; recovered diving rocks use AL.DiveExpansion.rockQuote. Residual food particles transfer explicitly at zero value. These rows do not convert the full displayed aquarium asset total into a promised payout.

Every resident uses the ordinary Aquarium Store offer: floor(the same actual Deep reference×0.60). The October20 instruction expressly applies this ordinary resale rule to all origins, superseding the earlier diving-only discount wording. Individual health, fictional/prohibited status, treatment, maturation, fragment establishment and other sale holds remain. Existing livestock dealer budget and individual daily sale counts apply cumulatively to livestock in the bundle; shell/equipment are physical buybacks, not a way to bypass the animal dealer budget. Living eggs/nurseries and retained brood remains have no authorized ordinary quote and block the sale until resolved. Ineligible animals are never silently discarded. Active receiving assignments to this aquarium also block sale until resolved/reassigned.

Core executes the whole sale on one validated draft. It either removes the owning tank plus its physical contents and pays the itemized total once, or rejects without deleting/paying anything. Uninstalled equipment, replacement-part quantities, objects, supplies, recovered rocks in storage and other tanks remain unchanged. One durable whole-aquarium:<tank ID> Finance key and receipt prevent resale/reload duplication. The selected tank falls back to another owned tank; selling the last tank saves an actual empty array and blank selection, with working settings, history, Inventory, Casino Profile and new-aquarium purchasing. No phantom tank or mandatory replacement purchase is created.

Discoveries/achievements are retained. Tank History keeps the sale, itemized IDs/offers, prior keeper events, resident health observations, equipment maintenance and value history; chemistry records and reproductive history remain saved. Breeding journals may reference a sold tank only through a validated sold-tank receipt; current courtship closes factually as transferred, not as death. No sold resident becomes a ghost owned animal or can be restored by ordinary reload. Deliberate offline JSON/client tampering is outside local anti-replay guarantees.
